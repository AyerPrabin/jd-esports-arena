Download for Android ✓ You're using the app
  1. Tap Download, then open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (only this once).
  3. Tap Install, open JD Arena and sign in. If Google Play Protect stops it, that's only because the app is new and not from the Play Store:
    • "Play Protect hasn't seen this app" → tap Scan app, wait a few seconds, then Install.
    • "Send app for a security check" → tap Send (or Don't send), then Install.
    • "Unsafe app blocked" → tap More details → Install anyway.
    The app asks for notifications, and (only if you allow it) your rough area for regional stats - never SMS, contacts or accessibility.
  4. Allow notifications so room IDs and results reach you.
  5. Had the old web version on your home screen? Delete it: long-press the old JD Arena icon → Uninstall (or Remove), so alerts come through the app and not twice.

Already have the app? Download again and tap Update - your account stays signed in.

It's the same JD Arena as the website, so it's always up to date. When a new app version comes out, the app shows an Update now button.

What is JD Arena?

JD Esports Arena is a Free Fire tournament app and website for players in Nepal: squad, duo and solo custom-room tournaments, 1v1 to 4v4 Clash Squad challenges, a season leaderboard and a points wallet.

Is the app free?

Yes, the app is free to download. Free events need a free card or 15 points; paid events have bigger prize pools (for example 50 points entry for a Rs 500 prize).

How do I get the room ID and password?

They arrive in the app as a notification, in Alerts and on your ticket about 5 minutes before the match.

How are prizes paid?

Prizes go straight into your JD wallet when results are published, split 50 / 30 / 20 for 1st, 2nd and 3rd. You can withdraw to eSewa from 100 points.
