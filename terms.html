5. Fair play

Never allowed:

  • Cheats, hacks, aimbots, wallhacks, macros or any modified game app.
  • Exploiting bugs — in Free Fire, on this site or in our systems — to gain points, prizes, places or access you shouldn't have. If you find a bug, tell us instead; we'd rather know.
  • Attacking, scraping, flooding or trying to break into the site, database, Discord bot or ZULU, or trying to get other players' private data (room IDs, emails, payment details).
  • Pretending to be another player, squad or JD Arena staff.
  • Match-fixing, boosting or teaming up with opponents.

What happens: a permanent ban for the account and any linked accounts, listed publicly on the ban list; any prize and points are forfeited. Serious attacks may be reported to the platforms involved or to the authorities.

Cheating in a paid tournament: once confirmed after review, every other squad in that tournament gets its entry fee refunded, the cheater's own entry fee is kept (not refunded), and their account is banned for good.

6. Entry fees & prizes

  • Each tournament's prize is a fixed amount, posted before registration opens. Winners receive the full posted prize with no deductions.
  • Entry fees are kept by JD Arena. They are not added to the prize — they pay for prizes (including prizes for free-entry tournaments), hosting and running costs.
  • When a winner is announced, the prize is paid into the winning squad's points wallet automatically (to the player who registered the squad on the site). Squads that didn't register on the site are paid by hand after contacting us.
  • Prizes for banned or disqualified players or squads are forfeited.
  • Fake or edited payment screenshots are fraud: the account is banned and any prize and points are forfeited.

7. Points wallet

  • 1 point = Rs 1. Points pay for paid entries and squad effects. They have no value outside JD Arena and don't earn interest.
  • Loading points: pay our eSewa QR with your JD Arena username in the payment remarks, then send the screenshot to us on WhatsApp. Points are added once we've verified the payment, usually within a few hours.
  • Withdrawing points: request a withdrawal to your own eSewa account from your wallet at any time. The points leave your wallet straight away and we send the money by hand, usually within a day. If we can't send it, the points are returned. You're responsible for giving correct eSewa details — money sent to details you entered wrongly may not be recoverable.
  • To protect everyone's points, the system can pause payments, withdrawals and codes while we investigate a possible security problem. Your balance is kept safe during a pause, and free tournaments carry on as normal.

9. Squad effects & codes

  • Squad effects are optional and cosmetic only — they change how your squad looks on the site and give no advantage in the game.

  • Effects are not refundable once activated.
  • Join codes and effect codes are single-use. An entry code only works for the tournament it was issued for, and only confirms your slot if one is still free — if not, the code stays unused and we'll sort it out with you.
