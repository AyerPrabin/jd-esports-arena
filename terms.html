Terms & Conditions

Last updated 2026 · Applies to jdesport.co.uk and the JD Esports Arena app, run solo by Prabin Ayer (AyerFire).

1. Who this covers

By creating an account, registering a squad, or otherwise using JD Esports Arena ("JD Arena"), you agree to these terms. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian should review this with you.

2. Accounts

  • One account per person. Multiple accounts used to re-enter a tournament, dodge a ban, or get extra chances at prizes will be banned.
  • You're responsible for what happens under your account, including your Discord link and anyone you share your login with.
  • Sign in with Google or email/password. Whichever you use, keep it secure — we can't undo actions taken by someone who got into your account.

3. Fair play — no cheating, hacking, or exploiting

This is the core rule. Not allowed, ever:

  • Cheats, hacks, aimbots, wallhacks, macros, or any modified game client/APK.
  • Exploiting a bug in the game, in JD Arena's website/app, or in Supabase/our backend to gain points, prizes, registrations, or account access you shouldn't have. If you find one, report it to us instead — we'd genuinely rather know.
  • Attempting to break into, scrape, flood, or otherwise attack JD Arena's systems (the site, the database, the Discord bot, the AI assistant). This includes trying to pull other players' private data (room IDs, emails, payment screenshots) through the API or anywhere else it isn't meant to be public.
  • Impersonating another player, squad, or JD Arena staff.
  • Match-fixing, boosting, or colluding with an opponent to manipulate results.

Violating this gets your account and any linked accounts banned, and any prize forfeited — see the public ban list. We may also report serious abuse (e.g. attacks on our systems) to the relevant platforms or authorities.

4. Registration & check-in

  • Squad names, logos, and screenshots you upload must be your own and not offensive, impersonating, or infringing on someone else's rights.
  • Show up and check in on time. No-shows can lose their slot to the waitlist.
  • An unverified account that never confirms its email is automatically cleaned up after a few days, freeing the slot for someone else.

5. Prizes & payments

  • Entry fees (for paid tournaments) and prize payouts are handled manually — see each tournament's listed entry/prize terms.
  • Payment screenshots are reviewed before a paid registration is confirmed; fake or altered screenshots are treated as fraud and banned.
  • Prizes for banned or disqualified players/squads are forfeited.

6. Your data

  • We store what's needed to run tournaments: your account info, registrations, and match results. Room IDs and other match-sensitive info are only ever shown to the players registered in that match. We don't sell your data.
  • Your username is the unique ID we and other players use to find and reference you — on rosters, in Discord linking, in reports — instead of your email, which is never shown to other players.
  • We take reasonable precautions, but no online system is 100% secure. We can't guarantee your data will never be exposed by a bug, breach, or attack outside our control, and we're not liable for damages arising from one.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings — this permanently removes your account after you confirm via email.

7. Reports & bans

Anyone can report a player or squad for suspected cheating or misconduct. Reports stay private during review; only an actual resulting ban becomes public, on the ban list, with a reason.

8. Changes

These terms can be updated as JD Arena grows. Meaningful changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions, disputes, or something to report? Use Report a player/squad in the app, or reach out via the About Us page.
