Who I Am

The person behind the system

I'm Prabin Ayer — a Computing Science student at Coventry University London (graduating July 2027) and a hospitality professional working London's venues as a bartender and manager. Born in Nepal, building in London, operating across both worlds at once.

I don't build for résumés. I build things that solve real problems in my own life. ZULU started as a WhatsApp automation bot and became a full tactical AI operating system — autonomous agents, semantic memory, self-rewriting code, all running locally on my own machine.

Alongside it I run AyerFire, a Free Fire esports content brand, and operate JD Esports Arena Nepal v2 — a real tournament platform with live leaderboards serving the Nepali Free Fire community. Everything I touch runs on real-world data: real players, real matches, real feedback.

Speaking English, Hindi, and Nepali lets me operate across borders — which matters, because the plan stretches from a London bar to a mountain estate in Sudurpashchim.

My Ideas · The Long Game

A conglomerate, built in phases

This isn't a daydream — it's a sequenced plan with a real unfair advantage: a multi-acre mountain estate in Dadeldhura, Nepal, rich in raw materials most manufacturers have to import. Each phase funds and feeds the next.

Phase 1 · Mid-2028
Jhalari Chemical Foundation
High-volume B2B and retail cleaning chemicals — dishwasher, floor cleaner, hand wash, toilet cleaner, air freshener — in 500ml, 1L and 2L formats. The edge: harvesting raw Sapindus mukorossi (Ritha) straight from the Dadeldhura estate to bypass expensive imported surfactants, powering a high-margin "Elite Tier" hybrid-eco line.
Phase 2–3 · 2029–2030
Autonomous Margin Protection
A private fleet of EV commercial trucks to dominate the highway supply chain and lock in distribution margins. ZULU expands into a full enterprise tracker — monitoring raw material stocks, EV routes, and B2B client purchase volumes automatically.
Phase 4–5 · 2031–2033
JD Pure Estate
Converting the Dadeldhura mountain property into a high-altitude organic extraction site. The product: ultra-premium, cold-pressed Himalayan vitality juice bottled at source — moving from low-cost chemicals into high-margin luxury health.
Phase 6 · 2034–2035+
JD Luxury Group
Ultra-premium timepieces — Swiss/Japanese automatic movements, Dadeldhura mountain-stone dials, local leather, 18K gold and diamond accents. The strategy: the 5-Lakh "Founders Edition" isn't for sale — it's unlocked only by B2B clients who buy 100,000 boxes of JD chemicals. A loyalty trap that secures permanent institutional clients, with a founder-led media play driving the brand.
