Get a free card for free tournaments

Every free tournament asks for a free card. Your first card comes from following us, one time only. After that, every free card needs a video about JD Arena. A JD mod checks it, and each card joins one free tournament. Don't want to? You can pay 15 points when you join instead.

  1. Follow, comment, repost 3 videos (one time only)

    On TikTok, open @ayer_fire and do all 3 below, then send your TikTok username. One card per player, once.

    ✓ You already got this card. For another free card, make a video about JD Arena below.

    Most refused requests miss one of these: not following yet, a comment without the JD username, or no repost. Use your exact TikTok username so the mod can find you.

  2. Make a video about JD Arena

    Make your own public video and say how to download the app: jdesport.co.uk/download. Send the link to your video. Photos and text posts don't count. Each video earns one card.

    Video kit: what to say and post

    15 to 30 seconds is enough. Record your screen with the JD Arena app open and say the link out loud.

🛡️ We are looking for mods. Promote JD Arena: our best promoters get a chance to become a JD mod.

Got 1,000+ views? Win bigger cards

Make a video or post about JD Esports Arena. When it passes 1,000 views, send us the link and get a free-entry card. 10,000+ views? Your card goes into a lucky draw for Silver or Gold.

🥉 BronzeRs 5 entry🥈 SilverRs 50 entry🥇 GoldRs 100 entry

  1. Post it

    TikTok, YouTube, Facebook or Instagram: your match clips, a win, or why you play here. Show the app or say jdesport.co.uk.

  2. Tag AyerFire

    Tag @ayer_fire and write both #AyerFire and #JDEsports in the caption, so we can find it.

  3. Reach 1,000 views

    Real views only. Bought or fake views get the post rejected and can get the account banned.

  4. Send the link

    Open your video in a browser and copy the full link (like tiktok.com/@you/video/123…). Short share links can't be checked.

A JD mod opens your link and checks the views and the tag, usually within a day. One card per video, up to 3 videos per player. Every card is Bronze unless the video has 10,000+ views, then it's a lucky draw (almost always Bronze, very rarely Silver or Gold). A card pays one entry up to its value, so a Bronze card works on Rs 5 events. A cheaper event is fine, but there's no change back. Cards last 30 days and are never cash or points. If you withdraw before the start, the free entry comes back.
