How results work
  • After the match, both captains tap We won or We lost. Tapping We lost pays the other side straight away.
  • If one side says they won and the other doesn't answer within 2 hours, the winner is paid automatically.
  • If both sides say they won, the admin decides — share screenshots in the challenge chat.
  • No one accepts within 24 hours, or nobody reports within 12 hours of accepting: stakes are refunded.
  • The winner gets both stakes minus a 1-point platform fee per player (e.g. 50 each → winner gets 98). Cancelled, expired or refunded challenges have no fee.
  • Cheating or lying about a result = stake forfeited and a ban. Stakes 10–500 points, 18+ only.
