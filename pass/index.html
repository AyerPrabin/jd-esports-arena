Good to know

No pass and we win?

You still get your prize: 50 points of it pay for a Weekly Pass (7 days), so the next wins that week are yours in full.

I bought before 27 Oct

Your 7 days start on Tue 27 Oct, 12:00 AM NPT. Prizes from tournaments before then are paid in full anyway.

Upgrading to Pro

Before 27 Oct you pay only the 30-point difference. After that, the days left on your pass turn into Pro days (50/80 of the time).

Invite friends

Share your JD tag from your profile. When a friend you invited wins their first prize, you both get +40 points.

Paid tournaments

Nothing changes: entry is paid with points and prizes are paid in full.

Rules still apply

A pass doesn't skip the rules. No-shows and banned skills can still lose you your slot.
