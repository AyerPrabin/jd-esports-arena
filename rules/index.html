Tournament rules

Play fair. Play together.

Every squad agrees to these rules before getting a slot

These rules apply to every JD Esports Arena tournament. Breaking one can get your squad disqualified from the match and banned from future tournaments.

The rules

1Full squad only

Entering the room without your squad is banned. Join the custom room together with your registered squad. A player who enters alone, without the squad, will be removed.

✅ Allowed
🧑🧑🧑🧑

One player enters the room alone, without the squad.

2Banned character skills

You can't use these characters' skills: Orion, Sonia, Dimitri and Ryden. Don't equip them for tournament matches.

3No duplicate active skills

Two players in the same squad can't use the same active skill. Every player in your squad needs a different active skill.

4No zone pusher

Zone pusher is not allowed. Play the safe zone normally and move in with the circle like everyone else.

5Enter the room within 10 minutes

Your squad must be in the room within 10 minutes of the Room ID and password being shared. Squads that aren't in by then can be left out of that match.

6Sit in your app slot

Your squad enters together and takes the seat that matches its slot position in the JD Arena app. Don't take another squad's seat.

7Join and don't come = ban

Registering means you'll play. Every time your squad registers and doesn't show up, it gets a warning. 3 no-shows = your squad is banned for 1 month.

8Register only if you'll be there

Register only if you'll attend the custom room and stay online in the JD Arena app. The Room ID, password and your slot are sent to the app as notifications.

📲 Download the JD Arena app (APK)

Also remember

  • Room settings are the same every match: gun attributes off, character skills on (except the banned ones), full map with the normal zone.
  • Points: each match your squad gets placement points from the official room result plus 1 point for every kill. All matches add up and the highest total wins.
  • Position points: 1st 12, 2nd 9, 3rd 8, 4th 7, 5th 6, 6th 5, 7th 4, 8th 3, 9th 2, 10th 1, 11th–12th 0. Kills: +1 point each. Example: 1st with 5 kills + 3rd with 2 kills = (12 + 5) + (8 + 2) = 27 points.
  • No cheating. Hacks, mod menus or aimbots mean a permanent ban, listed on the Ban List.
