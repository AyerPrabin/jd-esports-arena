The rules

1Full squad only

Entering the room without your squad is banned. Join the custom room together with your registered squad. A player who enters alone, without the squad, will be removed.

2Banned character skills

You can't use these characters' skills: Orion, Sonia, Dimitri and Ryden. Don't equip them for tournament matches.

3No duplicate active skills

Two players in the same squad can't use the same active skill. Every player in your squad needs a different active skill.

4No zone pusher

Zone pusher is not allowed. Play the safe zone normally and move in with the circle like everyone else.

5Enter the room within 10 minutes

Your squad must be in the room within 10 minutes of the Room ID and password being shared. Squads that aren't in by then can be left out of that match.

6Sit in your app slot

Your squad enters together and takes the seat that matches its slot position in the JD Arena app. Don't take another squad's seat.

7Join and don't come = ban

Registering means you'll play. Every time your squad registers and doesn't show up, it gets a warning. 3 no-shows = your squad is banned for 1 month.

8Register only if you'll be there

Register only if you'll attend the custom room and stay online in the JD Arena app. The Room ID, password and your slot are sent to the app as notifications.

📲 Download the JD Arena app (APK)

9Respect everyone — no rude language

No insults, swearing, slurs or threats: in the custom room, in JD Arena chat, or about other players anywhere.

Rude words in JD Arena chat are hidden automatically (***) and you get a reminder. Repeated or serious abuse can get your account banned (the admin decides). Lost a match? Say gg and come back stronger.

10The owner plays for fun

JD Arena's owner sometimes joins a tournament just for fun, playing from a laptop (PC emulator) with the same rules and no special treatment.

The owner never takes prize money. If the owner's squad finishes in a prize place, that prize goes to the next squad and every prize place below moves up one. For example, if the owner finishes 1st, the 2nd squad gets the 1st prize.

11Free tournaments: a free card or 15 points

Every free tournament needs one free card, or 15 points when you join. Without a card or points, you can't stay in a free tournament.

  1. First card (one time only): follow @ayer_fire, then comment on and repost 3 of our videos with your JD username in each comment.
  2. Every card after that: make a video about JD Arena that says how to download it.
  3. Send it in Wallet → Free entry. A mod checks it and gives you one card. One card joins one free tournament.
  4. Or invite friends: when a friend you invited plays their first event, you get a free card. Up to 3 friends.

We are looking for mods. Our best promoters get a chance to become a JD mod.

12How the prize pool is shared

Every JD tournament pays 50% to 1st, 30% to 2nd and 20% to 3rd. Each event card shows the exact amounts. For example, a Rs 180 pool pays Rs 90, Rs 54 and Rs 36.

Free tournaments have a Rs 180 prize. The big prizes are in paid tournaments. A partner host's event is paid by that host.

13Your wallet PIN

You need a 6-number wallet PIN to load points or withdraw. Create it in your wallet. Never share it: JD staff will never ask for it. Forgot it? Tap Forgot PIN? and verify with your Google account.

14Top slots and the free waitlist

Squads with a bought effect get the top slots. Other squads move down, keep their place in the event and get a message with their new slot.

In free tournaments, a place from the waitlist costs 20 points. If the event is full, a squad that hasn't confirmed, or else the newest squad, loses its place and gets its points or card back. So confirm early.

Paid events and partner-host events have a paid waitlist. When one is full, joining the waitlist costs half the entry. If a slot opens you move up automatically and the rest is taken from your wallet. If you don't have the points, the slot goes to the next squad. If no slot opens, or you leave the waitlist, your whole deposit comes back.

15Invite friends

Share your invite code from your profile. A new player types it when they create their account, or opens your invite link. When they save their own Free Fire UID and play an event they joined with 15 points (not a free card), you get one free entry card. It joins one free event and lasts 30 days.

You can invite up to 3 friends. Your own second account doesn't count: the same Free Fire UID never earns a card. Invites no longer pay points.

16Play with your registered line-up

Only the players in your squad's line-up on JD Arena can play. Need a substitute? Change your line-up in the app before the room opens. An unregistered player in your squad can cost your squad its points and prize.

17One account, one squad per event

One JD account per person, and one squad per player in each event. You can't play for two squads in the same tournament, or use a second account to get another slot, dodge a ban or win more prizes.

18No teaming

Every squad plays for itself. Working with another squad, letting enemies go on purpose, sharing positions or splitting a prize with another squad is teaming.

🚫 Two squads agree not to fight each other, or one squad feeds kills to another.
✅ Fight every squad you meet. Third-partying a fight is fine: that's normal play.

Teaming = both squads disqualified, prizes taken back and a ban.

19Keep the Room ID private

The Room ID and password are only for your squad. Don't post them in groups, on stream or anywhere else. A squad that shares them, or brings in anyone who isn't registered, is disqualified from that match.

20No glitches or bug abuse

Using a game bug to win is not allowed: hiding under the map, inside rocks or walls, in places players can't normally reach, or any trick that makes you impossible to hit. That squad loses that match's points. Found a bug? Tell a mod instead.

21Devices and apps

Phones, tablets and PC emulators are all allowed in tournaments. Not allowed: hacks, mod menus, modified game files, macros or auto-clickers, and any app that shows enemies or changes how the game plays. Apps that only change your phone or PC performance settings are fine.

Clash Squad challenges are different: challenges are mobile only.

22Disconnects and restarts

A match isn't restarted because one squad or player disconnected. Get back in if the game lets you. The host only restarts a match if the room crashes or the host made a setup mistake, and says so in the room chat and the app.

23Results and ties

Results come from the official room result: position points plus 1 point per kill. If two squads have the same total, the tie is broken in this order:

  1. More Booyahs (1st places)
  2. More kills in total
  3. Better place in the last match

24Complaints and staff decisions

Saw a rule broken? Report it with proof (screenshot or recording) using Report a player, or message a JD mod, within 1 hour of the match, before results are published if you can. Prizes are paid as soon as results are published.

The host's and JD mods' decision is final. Arguing politely is fine; abuse towards staff is treated like rule 9. False reports to get another squad disqualified can get you banned.

Also remember

  • Room settings are the same every match: gun attributes off, character skills on (except the banned ones), full map with the normal zone. With gun attributes off, every gun uses its base stats whatever skin you have.
  • Points: each match your squad gets placement points from the official room result plus 1 point for every kill. All matches add up and the highest total wins.
  • Position points: 1st 12, 2nd 9, 3rd 8, 4th 7, 5th 6, 6th 5, 7th 4, 8th 3, 9th 2, 10th 1, 11th–12th 0. Kills: +1 point each. Example: 1st with 5 kills + 3rd with 2 kills = (12 + 5) + (8 + 2) = 27 points.
  • No cheating. Hacks, mod menus or aimbots mean a permanent ban, listed on the Ban List.

Clash Squad challenge rules

These rules are for every 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 and 4v4 Clash Squad challenge. Creating or accepting a challenge means you agree to them, and to the note and room settings shown on that challenge.

  • 📱Mobile onlyNo laptop, PC or emulator
  • ⚙️Use the challenge's room settingsEverything else stays on default: no 1 HP rooms
  • 📝The note is a rule"No Oscar" means no Oscar
  • 🪪Only the real playersYour own Free Fire UID, no stand-ins
  • 🚪Leaving the match = you loseFinish every match you start
  • ⚖️Break a rule: warning, fine, lock or banFines go to the player you cheated

C1Mobile only

Every player plays on a phone or tablet. A laptop, PC or emulator (BlueStacks, MSI and so on) is not allowed in challenges.

C2The room must match the challenge

Whoever makes the custom room uses exactly the settings shown on the challenge: character skills, gun attributes, ammo, gloo walls, rounds (first to 4 or first to 7) and the map.

Everything the challenge doesn't show stays on the Free Fire default: HP, EP, movement speed, jump height, starting coins, revive and the zone. Changing a hidden setting to win (for example a 1 HP room) is cheating.

The challenge creator makes the room, unless both sides agree in the chat that the other side makes it. Before round 1, the room maker sends a screenshot of the room settings in the challenge chat with 📷. No settings screenshot and a hidden setting turns out wrong? The room maker is treated as the rule breaker.

Check the room before round 1. If something is wrong, say it in the challenge chat and don't start. Playing on means you accept the room, except for a hidden setting you couldn't see.

C3The challenge note is a rule

If the challenge has a note, both sides must follow it, for example "Desert Eagle and M1887 only" or "no Oscar". Accepting the challenge means you accept the note. A note can add rules, but it can't cancel a JD rule.

🚫 The note says no Oscar and a player uses Oscar.
✅ Read the note before you accept. Not happy with it? Don't accept.

C4Banned characters

With character skills on, the tournament-banned characters are banned in challenges too: Orion, Sonia, Dimitri and Ryden. The note can ban more characters or guns.

C5Only the real players

Every player uses the Free Fire UID saved on their own JD profile. No stand-ins, no playing on someone else's account, and the line-up can't change after the match starts.

C6Starting, disconnects and proof

  1. Share the Room ID in the challenge chat and start within 15 minutes of accepting.
  2. A disconnect doesn't stop the match: the round counts. A restart only happens if both captains agree in the chat.
  3. Take a screenshot of the final result. If you think a rule was broken, also screenshot it (the room settings, the character, the gun) and send it in the chat with 📷.
  4. Then report the result: We won, We lost or 🚩 They broke the rule.

C7Play fair

No hacks, mod menus, aimbots, panels or bugs used on purpose. No arranged results and no using challenges to move points between accounts. Any of these = stake lost and a permanent ban, listed on the Ban List. Be respectful in the challenge chat.

C8Nobody else in the room

Only the challenge players join the room. No spectators, observers or extra players, unless both sides agree in the chat first.

C9Leaving the match = you lose

Leaving on purpose, giving up or not finishing the match counts as a loss. A player whose game crashes can come back in if the game allows it; the match goes on (see C6).

C10Guns, items and glitches

Every gun and item you can buy in the round is allowed, unless the note bans it. Map glitches, hiding in walls or places you can't normally reach, and any bug used to win are not allowed.

C11Honest results, no side deals

Report the real result. Lying about a result = your stake goes to the other side and a challenge ban.

All stakes go through JD. Side bets or payments outside JD aren't protected, and paying or asking someone to lose is match-fixing: both players are banned.

C12What happens if a rule is broken

Tap 🚩 They broke the rule before the result, or up to 2 hours after it, and send your proof. The winnings are held while a JD mod checks the chat and screenshots. If the rule was broken:

  • The win is cancelled and both stakes are refunded.
  • The rule breaker can be fined up to 20 points, which go to the player who reported. The mod picks the amount, usually 10 points.
  • The mod can also lock their challenges for 1, 3, 7 or 30 days, or ban them from challenges for good. Hacking is a full account ban.

False or pointless reports are punished too: the second rejected report in 30 days locks your challenges for 2 days.
