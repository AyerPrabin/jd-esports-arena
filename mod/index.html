📖 How to use this panel (tap to open)

1 · Send an event notification
Pick the event (the nearest one is already chosen). Tap a quick template, or type your own title and message. Choose who gets it — All players (to promote the event) or only registered squads (for room IDs / reminders). Press Send. Players get a push alert on their phone, plus an in-app notification.

2 · Contact a player
Tap an event under “Today & upcoming”, then scroll to Squads & reminders. Each squad’s WhatsApp number is a green button — tap it to open WhatsApp and message them directly (great for room-ID reminders).

3 · How the point system works (so you can answer players)
1 point = Rs 1. Players load points on their Wallet by paying the eSewa QR and uploading the screenshot — the admin checks it and adds the points. Points pay for paid-event entries, squad effects and the JD-Optimiers app. Only the admin adds or removes points — moderators can’t change balances.

What you can do: send event notifications, view events, message squads. What you can’t: money/points, bans, editing events or plans — those stay with the admin.
