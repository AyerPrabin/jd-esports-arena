Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026 · Covers jdesport.co.uk, the installable app, the Discord bot and the ZULU assistant.

The short version
  • We only collect what we need to run tournaments, the points wallet and challenges.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. We never sell your data.
  • Payment screenshots are deleted 30 days after we check them.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time from your account settings.

1. Who we are

JD Esports Arena is run by an individual, Prabin Ayer (trading as AyerFire), based in the United Kingdom, for players in Nepal and elsewhere. He decides how your data is used. Contact: prabinayer7@gmail.com.

3. Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run JD Arena. Each processes data for us under its own privacy terms:

  • Supabase — accounts, database and server functions
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website
  • Google — optional "Continue with Google" sign-in, and the website fonts (Google Fonts)
  • jsDelivr — delivers one code library the site uses
  • Resend — sends emails
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla) — delivers alerts if you turn them on
  • Discord — only if you link your account
  • Google Gemini and Groq — AI services that answer ZULU questions. They receive the question you type, never your account data.
  • eSewa — you pay through eSewa directly; we never see your eSewa login

Other players can see your username, squad name, logo and results. They never see your email, WhatsApp number or payment details.

4. Cookies and local storage

We don't use cookies for tracking or advertising. The site stores a few things in your browser so it works:

  • Your sign-in session (Supabase) — keeps you logged in
  • Language (English or Nepali) and the name you gave ZULU
  • Your eSewa ID and name — only after you request a withdrawal, so you don't have to type them again

These are strictly necessary for features you use, so we don't ask for consent for them. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings; you'll be logged out.

5. Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the Terms and this policy for you and should handle any payments, withdrawals and prizes. Challenges with points stakes are 18+ only. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

6. Your rights

  • See or copy your data — most of it is on your profile and wallet pages; for a full copy, email us.
  • Correct it — change your username, squad name and logo on your profile, or email us.
  • Delete it — see section 7.
  • Stop optional emails — use the "Unsubscribe" link in any reminder, results or announcement email. Room details, check-in notices and account emails still arrive because you need them to play.
  • Turn off push alerts or unlink Discord at any time.

We reply to requests within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

7. Deleting your account

Open your account settings and choose Delete account. We email you a confirmation link; once you click it, your account is deleted permanently together with your registrations, points history, withdrawals, payment screenshots, notifications, challenges and push subscriptions. Points still in your wallet are lost, so withdraw them first. Squad names in already-published results and public ban list entries are kept as part of the event record.

Can't sign in? Email prabinayer7@gmail.com from the address on the account and we'll delete it for you.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, passwords are hashed, and database rules only let you read your own private data. No online service is completely secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top changes, and important changes are shown when you next sign in.
