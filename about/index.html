JD Esports Arena

Free Fire BR tournaments · Nepal · by AyerFire

JD Esports Arena is a home for competitive Free Fire in Nepal — live brackets, real prize pools, instant room-ID delivery, and a season leaderboard, run for free. Squads join a tournament over WhatsApp, Discord, or an in-app account; room codes land as a push alert or email the moment a match is about to start. Built and run solo, under the AyerFire banner.
