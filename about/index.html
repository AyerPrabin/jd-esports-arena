JD Esports Arena

Free Fire BR tournaments · Nepal · by AyerFire

JD Esports Arena is a home for competitive Free Fire in Nepal — live brackets, real prize pools, instant room-ID delivery, and a season leaderboard. Prize pools are fixed and posted up front, and free-entry tournaments are funded by us. Squads join a tournament over WhatsApp, Discord, or an in-app account; room codes land as a push alert or email the moment a match is about to start. Built and run solo, under the AyerFire banner.

What you can do here

  • 🏆 TournamentsFree and paid Free Fire BR squad events with fixed prizes posted before sign-up, live slot counts, check-in and a ticket with your room ID and password about 5 minutes before the match. See events
  • 👥 Find TeammatesPost your role (rusher, sniper, supporter, IGL, bomber, all-rounder or your own), play free or for JD points, and team up with players for any event. Your Free Fire UID and in-game name are required, so squads know who they're playing with. Find a teammate
  • 💬 Chat & friendsAdd friends, message them, send photos and videos up to 25 MB (private to the two of you, kept in the app for offline viewing, not saved to your gallery), make squad chats and share room IDs. Open chat

  • 📊 Leaderboard & resultsSeason standings, match-by-match results and past events. Leaderboard

  • 🤖 ZULUOur AI assistant answers questions about events, rules, your tickets and points in seconds, and brings in the JD team for bigger problems.

How we keep it fair and safe

  • Fair play first. Cheats, hacks and mods mean a permanent ban and no prize. See the ban list and rules.
  • Every payment is checked by a person before points are added. A database rule stops a balance changing without a matching record.
  • Pay teammates only in JD points, inside the app. Never send eSewa or cash between players.
  • Your private data stays private. Your email and WhatsApp number are never shown to other players, and room IDs only go to registered squads.
  • Report and block anyone who breaks the rules. Our moderators review reports.
