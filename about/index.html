How a match night goes

  1. Join the event. Pick it on Events and pay with a free card or points. Your seat shows on the slot list.
  2. Save your Free Fire ID. Profile, Free Fire ID card: your UID and in-game name. Hosts check it in the room.
  3. Get the room. About 5 minutes before the start, the room ID and password come as a phone alert and on your ticket.
  4. Play. Join the custom room in Free Fire with the UID you saved.
  5. Get paid. Results go on the leaderboard and the prize is added to the winners' wallets as points (1 point = Rs 1).

What we promise

  • If an event is cancelled, you get everything back: your card or 100% of your points, with the reason shown on the event.
  • A host who doesn't run the room is removed. Every player in that event gets their card or points back.
  • No points without a real payment. Each load is matched to an eSewa payment, automatically or by a person, and a database rule stops any balance changing without a record.
  • Fair play. Hacks, cheats and mods mean a permanent ban and no prize. See the ban list and rules.
  • Your details stay private. Other players never see your email or phone number, and room IDs only go to players in that event.

The full details are in the Terms and Privacy Policy.
