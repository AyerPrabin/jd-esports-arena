Reviews moderate · public trust feature

Anyone who was confirmed/approved for a tournament can leave a public star rating + comment once it's marked "completed" — no pre-approval queue, but you can remove an abusive or spam one here after the fact.

Reports private until actioned

Players can report a squad/player (with an optional evidence link) for anything — emulator misdeclaration, cheating, no-shows. Reports are never public; only a ban resulting from one shows up on the public ban list.

Ban List public · blocks re-registration

Banning someone here (via a report above, or directly) actually blocks their account from registering for future tournaments — not just a name on a page. The list itself (tag/username, reason, date — never contact info) is public.

Anomaly Flags cheating signal · never auto-bans

When you archive results, any squad whose kills spike well past their own season average (needs at least 2 prior appearances to judge) gets flagged here automatically. This never bans or blocks anyone by itself — it's just a "look at this" signal. Review the replay/context yourself, then use Reports/Ban List above if action is warranted.

Players buy plans with points on /optimizer/; the Windows app only opens with an active plan. Max also unlocks Panel scan, a 30-day Pro squad effect per purchase, early entry and the ✔ tag. Give free days here (sponsors, refunds, testers), end a plan, or unlink a PC. 2 PCs per account.

Moderators staff · host

Mods sign in at /mod/ with their JD account (add them by that email). Staff = your own mods: every event, JD Esports chat, prize payouts. Host = an invited scrim host: creates and runs only their own free events (or ones whose "Host mod handle" you set) – squads, room code, notifications to those squads, results. Everything they send is signed with their @handle; JD never pays their prizes. No JD Esports chat. Only you can notify all players. Nobody can touch bans, plans or balances.

🏆 MEGA event big paid event · refund if it doesn't fill

One huge event (e.g. 1,500 squads, 200 points entry, Rs 3,00,000). Players join and pay like any event. If fewer than Min squads have paid when registration closes, everyone is refunded automatically and it's called off; otherwise it's confirmed and invite rewards are paid (host mods per squad they brought, players per squad they invited). Every day at 7 PM players who haven't joined get a progress alert (they can turn it off). The page is /mega/. Set the start date later in Tournaments; qualifier groups are drawn when registration closes.
