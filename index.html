How to join

Tutorial
  1. 1
    Sign upCreate a free account. Your squad name and logo are saved for every event.
  2. 2
    Load pointsIn your wallet, pay the eSewa QR and upload the payment screenshot. Points are added once we check it (1 point = Rs 1).Open wallet →
  3. 3
    Join with pointsTap Join on an event. Free events confirm straight away; paid entries are taken from your points.
  4. 4
    Confirm & playAfter you register, tap "Confirm you're playing" on the event. The window opens 48h before start and closes 24h before — not confirmed by then means you lose your seat.
  5. 5
    Get your Room IDThe Room ID and password appear on your ticket about 5 minutes before the match.

How Points Work

Placement + kills

Each match, your squad gets placement points from the official room result plus 1 point for every kill.

Kill points1 point for every kill your squad gets in the match.
TotalPlacement points and kill points from all matches are added together. The highest total wins.

📊 Position points

Where your squad finishes in each match gives placement points. Every kill adds 1 more point.
